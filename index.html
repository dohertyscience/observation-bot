        How the bot scores

        The same rubric as your Virtual Trips slideshow
        Level
        Example
        5
        Expert (for 7th grade). Everything in level 4, plus richer precise detail: color, material, shape, parts, texture, sound, or motion. One clean sentence, no filler.
        Six pale tan stone buildings about 5 stories tall have rows of tall rectangular windows with black iron balconies.
        4
        Detailed. Several specific features blended with something quantitative: a size estimate, a time, or a comparison to something you know.
        There are six stone buildings about 5 stories tall which have rows of tall rectangular windows with their own balconies.
        3
        Solid. Several specific features: material, count, shape, parts. Objective and checkable, but no measurement or comparison yet.
        There are six stone buildings which have rows of tall rectangular windows with balconies.
        2
        Vague. One specific feature and nothing else.
        There are buildings which have tall rectangular windows.
        1
        Basic. A bare noun, or a noun with one generic word. A camera could record it, but it tells almost nothing.
        I see buildings. / There are buildings. / The water is blue.
        X
        Not an observation. Opinions, feelings, guesses, reasons, explanations, questions, or predictions. Fails the camera test and scores 1.
        This street is beautiful. / The people must be happy. / It was built for tourists.
        Level 5 is the best a 7th grader is expected to do in this assignment. Be highly descriptive, blend words with numbers or comparisons, and keep only what a camera could record.
